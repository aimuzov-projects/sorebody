body.glb is a simplified and compressed derivative of BodyParts3D.

BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en

Changes: skin, skeletal muscles and bones merged into three meshes; external genitalia removed from
the skin and the groin closed with a smooth patch; simplified with meshoptimizer, quantized and
compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression. This derivative is shared under the same license.
