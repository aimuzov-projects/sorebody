Changes: in body.glb, skin, skeletal muscles and bones are merged into three meshes. Each chunk in detail/ holds the
BodyParts3D bones, muscles, cartilage and vessels around one zone, plus Z-Anatomy ligaments, menisci, labra, joint
capsules, selected nerves and the muscles BodyParts3D lacks (latissimus dorsi, multifidus, quadratus lumborum,
internal oblique, transversus and rectus abdominis). Muscle vertices carry a muscle index and a fiber direction.
The Z-Anatomy parts are exported from Blender with modifiers applied and fitted to the BodyParts3D bones zone by zone
(ICP with scale); ligaments and nerves are then clipped to the BodyParts3D skin. Z-Anatomy parts under
non-commercial licenses (inner ear, kidney) are not used. All meshes are simplified with meshoptimizer, quantized and
compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression. body.glb is shared under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP. The chunks in detail/ combine both
sources and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, which BY-SA 2.1 JP allows as a later version with the same elements.
